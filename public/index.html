How to play
  • Aim: drag on the battlefield, or ← → angle, ↑ ↓ power (hold Shift for fine tuning)
  • Move: A / D (limited fuel each turn)
  • Weapons: 1–4   Fire: Space
  • Watch the wind arrow. Big blasts dig craters and drop tanks, and falls hurt.
